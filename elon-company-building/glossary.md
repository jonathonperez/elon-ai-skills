# Glossary

**The Algorithm** — Elon's five-step engineering process, in strict order: question requirements → delete → simplify → accelerate → automate (Ch 1)

**Attitude over skill** — hiring rule: skills can be taught; "attitude change requires a brain transplant" (Ch 6)

**Axiomatic base** — the set of foundational truths you reason up from in first-principles thinking (Ch 2)

**Burn-rate arithmetic** — judging every spend/delay against daily burn and future daily revenue; buy time, not convenience (Ch 3)

**Camaraderie is dangerous** — friendship stops teammates challenging each other's work; the judge, not the friend (Ch 1, Ch 6)

**Chief-engineer thinking** — everyone understands the whole system well enough to spot a bad optimization (Ch 6)

**Delete, delete, delete** — the reflexive drive to remove parts, process steps, and connections; "the best part is no part" (Ch 1, Ch 3)

**Eating glass** — working on the problems the company needs, not the ones you want (Ch 8)

**Epoch-making framing** — casting the work as civilization-scale so people sprint for it (Ch 7)

**First-principles thinking** — reasoning up from physics and material truths instead of by analogy to precedent (Ch 2)

**Frontline general** — leading physically from where the work happens; "nobody bleeds for the prince in the palace" (Ch 5)

**Fresh blood** — Elon's tolerance (preference) for churn over comfortable veterans (Ch 6)

**Fuzzy thinker** — someone whose asks are unclear; A-players won't work for one (Ch 6)

**Hand-on-the-stove principle** — pain must reach the decision-maker instantly; co-locate design with production (Ch 5)

**Hardcore / Ultra Hardcore** — the demanded intensity level; also the title of his Model S-era culture email (Ch 6)

**Idiot Index** — finished-product cost ÷ raw-material cost; high ratio = manufacturing waste to attack (Ch 2)

**"I am wired for war"** — his self-description, repeated across decades; extended calm unnerves him (Ch 8)

**Maniacal sense of urgency** — the operating principle; his most-repeated phrase (Ch 4)

**One dramatic demonstration** — replacing the pitch deck with the working thing (Ch 7)

**Phoning in rich** — long-tenured employees with money who no longer hunger for the factory floor (Ch 6)

**Physics is law** — the only immutable requirements; everything else is a recommendation (Ch 1, Ch 2)

**Polytopia lessons** — CEO rules derived from the strategy game: play life like a game, don't fear losing, optimize every turn, double down (Ch 8)

**Question every requirement** — Algorithm step 1; every requirement needs a named human owner (Ch 1)

**Reasoning by analogy** — the default human mode Elon rejects for important decisions (Ch 2)

**Repetition is persuasive** — repeating mantras until executives mouth the words along (Ch 1, Ch 4)

**Showmanship is salesmanship** — Senra's maxim for Elon's demo-driven selling (Ch 7)

**Shortest-path communication** — information flows directly between whoever needs it, never up the chain of command (Ch 5)

**Silent-thinking sessions** — zero-stimuli deep-thought blocks (lights off, motionless, hours) (Ch 8)

**Skip-level** — solving problems with the level below your managers (Ch 1, Ch 5)

**SR-71 principle** — speed as both offense and defense; the never-hit Blackbird (Ch 4)

**Theoretically perfect product** — define the physics-limit ideal, work backwards from it (Ch 2)

**Thinking in limits** — pushing a parameter to the edge of failure to find what can be deleted (Ch 2, Ch 3)

**Time as the only currency** — "okay to scrap equipment or money; not okay to scrap time" (Ch 4)

**Vertical integration** — in-house everything to control speed, cost, quality; outsourcing inherits legacy constraints (Ch 3)

**Walk to the red** — instrument stations green/red, then physically go to the red light and interrogate to root cause (Ch 5)

**Wouldn't-happen-without-us test** — dedicate yourself only to outcomes that fail without you (Ch 7)
